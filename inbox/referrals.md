# Referral board (built by /network-scout lens 3 — the alumni engine)

The point of this channel: **most companies are hiring and never post it, and there is no way to
find that out except asking someone inside.** So nothing here waits on a job posting. Pick IIIT
Nagpur seniors, ask each one whether their team is taking anyone on, and treat the answers as the
lead source.

Every message here is Shivam's to send himself. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## How to read a row

```
- **{Name}** — {role} @ {Company} | IIITN'{yy} | {2nd/3rd}° | {N} mutuals | state: {state} | {date} | {why}
```

States: `queued` → `invite-sent` → `accepted` → `ask-sent` → `replied` / `nudged` / `no-reply`
/ `closed`.

Why two stages: a senior is 2nd/3rd degree, and a free account can't DM a non-connection. So the
invite goes first (300-char note if LinkedIn offers the box, bare invite if it doesn't), and the
real ask goes out only once they accept. **The stage-2 ask is drafted at the same time as the
invite** and parked here, so accepting turns into a copy-paste instead of a writing job.

Rules that keep this channel alive:
- One person per company per cycle. Queue the second as backup.
- 8 invites per run, 15–20 per week, 5 stage-2 asks drafted per run. Acceptance rate beats volume.
- One nudge, at 7 days, only if they accepted the invite. Never a second one.
- Any answer is a win: a "yes we're hiring" gets promoted into `inbox/leads.md` scored A–F and
  tagged `(warm: {Name}, alumni referral)`. A "no" gets recorded on the row so the company isn't
  re-checked for a while.

Source for finding rows (verified 2026-07-30, 3,051 alumni, no profile visits needed):
`linkedin.com/school/indian-institute-of-information-technology-nagpur/people/?keywords={q}`
