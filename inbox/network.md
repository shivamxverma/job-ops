`- **{Name}** — {role/headline} @ {Company} | 1st° | relevance: {high/med/low + why one-liner} | linkedin.com/in/{slug}`

Use filtered 1st° people-search queries (`/search/results/people/?network=%5B%22F%22%5D` plus
`founder OR CTO OR "hiring" OR "engineering manager"`), never a full crawl of the connection list.
Profile anchors are not inside `<li>` elements: read `document.body.innerText` and collect
`a[href*="/in/"]` hrefs separately.
